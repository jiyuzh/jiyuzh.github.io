## About Me

{{< figure class="avatar" src="/profile.png" alt="avatar">}}

I'm a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I'm working with Professor [Tianyin Xu](https://tianyin.github.io/) and [Nam Sung Kim](https://ece.illinois.edu/about/directory/faculty/nskim) on improving the performance and scalability of modern cloud systems, with an emphasis on topics related to memory and storage systems.

I received my M.S. degree from UIUC in 2024 and my Bachelor's degree from the New Jersey Institute of Technology in 2022.

---

## Research Interest

My research lies at the intersection of operating systems and computer architecture, with a focus on memory, storage, and virtualization in cloud and data center environments. My work emphasis on improving performance, scalability, and resource efficiency of current and future systems, while preserving compatibility and application transparency.

Recently, I have been exploring CXL-based architectures for direct accelerator interoperability, disaggregated shared memory, and high-performance storage virtualization.
